Vortex Formation
Vorticity ω = ∇ × u measures the local rotation of fluid elements. In 3D, vorticity can be amplified by stretching — a vortex tube pulled thinner spins faster, conserving angular momentum.

This is the mechanism the Millennium Problem is asking about.
Key Result
Leray (1934): Weak solutions to 3D Navier–Stokes exist globally. Smoothness of these solutions remains unproven.
