What you are seeing

The colored links are SU(2) gauge variables U_μ(x) — one per lattice bond. The bright flux tube between the quarks (red/green dots) is real: it is the chromodynamic string that confines quarks inside protons. At β ≈ 2.3 (physical QCD regime) the string tension σ ≈ 0.18 in lattice units. Increase β → deconfinement. The string breaks.
