After activating, press and hold on the stage (3D view) to aim the burst (long-press works on mobile too). Just holding still bursts outward from the click point like a swirling vortex, spanning 180° around the tangential direction while keeping the same sense of rotation (counter-clockwise). Dragging while held adds that direction and distance as a shared velocity bias to every particle, giving the burst a lopsided, "aimed" push. Releasing fires it. The colors aren't random — they represent each particle's orbit (aphelion) computed from its initial conditions: red/orange = tight, deep orbits, purple = wide orbits, white = escaping (unbound) trajectories. Works regardless of the current experiment (mode).
