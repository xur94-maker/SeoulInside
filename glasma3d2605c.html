KEY INSIGHT
Classical Yang-Mills has no mass parameter — the action S = ∫ Tr(F∧★F)/g² is dimensionless. Yet quantization introduces ΛQCD via dimensional transmutation: the running coupling hits a pole at a finite scale, creating a dynamical mass gap. All glueball masses scale as m ∝ ΛQCD.
