CONNECTION TO YANG-MILLS
Every collision cross-section computed at the LHC uses α_s(μ) — the running coupling from the β function. At high √s, α_s → 0 (asymptotic freedom): quarks behave as nearly free particles inside the collision fireball. At low energies, α_s → ∞ near Λ_QCD: confinement kicks in and only hadrons emerge. The mass gap is why you never see a lone quark in the detector.
