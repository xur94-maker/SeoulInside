YANG-MILLS & MASS GAP
This simulator implements the 2-loop β-function for α_s(μ), PDF-sampled Monte Carlo for jet production, and Glauber modeling for heavy-ion collisions. The mass gap — why quarks are confined — emerges from non-perturbative QCD and is observed in lattice simulations (m_glueball ≈ 1.6 GeV). The Millennium Problem asks for a rigorous proof of the mass gap in 4D Yang-Mills theory. It remains UNSOLVED.
