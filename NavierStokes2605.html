What is Blow-Up?
The Navier–Stokes Millennium Problem asks: given smooth initial conditions, does the velocity field u(x,t) remain smooth for all time? A blow-up means the energy norm ‖u‖ → ∞ in finite time T*.

In 3D, nobody knows if this can happen. Lower ν (viscosity) → higher Reynolds number → turbulence → the boundary of what mathematics can currently guarantee. Your mission: push the system as close to singularity as possible.
