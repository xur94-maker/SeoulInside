❓ P vs. NP — The Most Important Open Problem in Computer Science

P = problems solvable in polynomial time (O(nᵏ) steps). Easy to solve.
NP = problems where solutions can be verified in polynomial time. Easy to check.

📊 The Question: P = NP or P ⊊ NP? If P = NP, every verifiable problem is solvable efficiently.
🔍 Most experts believe P ≠ NP (99% among active researchers). But no proof in 50+ years.

📜 The Three Barriers — Why No Proof Has Succeeded

Any proof of P ≠ NP must overcome all three barriers simultaneously.

1. Relativization (1975)
Baker–Gill–Solovay: Proofs that work relative to an oracle cannot resolve P vs NP.
2. Natural Proofs (1997)
Razborov–Rudich: "Natural" circuit lower bounds would break cryptography.
3. Algebrization (2009)
Aaronson–Wigderson: Even algebraic methods fail to separate P and NP.
📌 No known proof technique evades all three. The next breakthrough will require entirely new mathematics.
