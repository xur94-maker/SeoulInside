① Phase space: uncertainty region

The dashed circle is ordinary vacuum (equal spread). The ellipse is squeezed vacuum: same area, compressed in one direction. The uncertainty principle is unchanged.

② Pair structure: probability of n pairs

P(n) = (1−γ²)γ²ⁿ, γ = tanh r. Squeezed vacuum is a superposition of states in which particles come in pairs (Sec. 4.4.4).

③ The simplest example: an oscillator whose frequency suddenly changes (Sec. 4.4.5)

α = (ω₁+ω₂)/(2√ω₁ω₂)
|β| = |ω₂−ω₁|/(2√ω₁ω₂)
⟨N⟩ = (ω₁−ω₂)²/(4ω₁ω₂)
Matching r

For ω₂ = 2ω₁, ⟨N⟩ = 1/8. Particle creation by cosmic expansion or by rapidly changing boundaries (the dynamical Casimir effect) is the field version of this simple case.

The Unruh Effect: The Vacuum Seen by an Accelerating Observer

Move the sliders. The spacetime diagram, particle-number distribution, and thermometer respond together.

① Spacetime diagram (Rindler wedge)

The blue curve is the accelerated observer in the right wedge; the orange curve is its partner in the left wedge. The larger a is, the closer the curve hugs the horizon (dashed), and the observer can never see beyond the horizon.

② Particle-number distribution P(n)

The full state is a pure state in which L and R are always tied to the same n. For an R observer who cannot see L, it becomes a thermal distribution. That is what the Unruh temperature is.

③ Unruh temperature (log scale)

T_U = ħa/(2πck_B). At Earth gravity (9.8 m/s²) it is 4×10⁻²⁰ K, so it cannot be felt at all.

③ The Page curve: what happens to the radiation entropy during evaporation

Dashed: Hawking original calculation (a pure state becomes thermal radiation, losing information). Solid: the Page curve, assuming unitary evolution. How to Read This Page

Vacuum Interactive Gallery: five labs for playing with the equations and numbers of the "What Is a Vacuum?" series (B. Sun, Seoul Inside).

Every lab checks its numbers against the series text in its verification panel. Follow one question through all of them: is the vacuum really "nothing at all"? Note that the Unruh effect and Hawking radiation have not been directly observed, and whether the Casimir force or zero-point energy can be read as "evidence of vacuum energy" is still debated.
