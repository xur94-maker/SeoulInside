Experiment 02 — π(x) vs li(x): Prime Counting

Prime Counting Function vs. Logarithmic Integral

If the Riemann Hypothesis is true, the error between π(x) (actual prime count) and li(x) (logarithmic integral) is bounded by √x · log(x). This is why RH underpins modern cryptography.

Experiment 03 — Montgomery–Odlyzko Law: Random Matrices vs. Zero Spacings

GUE Random Matrices ↔ Zeta Zero Spacing Distribution

At Princeton's afternoon tea in 1972, mathematician Montgomery and physicist Dyson discovered they had independently derived the same formula. The energy level spacing of atomic nuclei = the zero spacing of the zeta function. This is why overwhelming numerical evidence exists — even without a proof.
