Use “Reverse velocities” to test the reversibility of Newtonian mechanics (Loschmidt’s paradox) yourself.

Positional entropy H / Hmax (per coarse-graining grid)

The solid line is the measured value; the dashed line is the expected value for a perfectly uniform random placement with the same N (including finite-sample bias). Equilibrium is reached when the solid line meets the dashed line. The vertical dashed line marks the moment of velocity reversal.

Boltzmann entropy S/kB = ln W

The logarithm of the number of microstates W = N! / ∏ nᵢ! for the occupation numbers (n₁, n₂, …) of the middle grid. It is an extensive quantity that grows in proportion to N; the dashed line is the equilibrium expectation.

Speed distribution vs 2D Maxwell–Boltzmann

Measured (distribution shaped by collisions)Theory (Rayleigh distribution)

The x-axis is speed/vrms and the y-axis is the bin probability. Since energy is conserved, the theoretical curve is recomputed from the current energy. If all particles start with the same speed, the histogram starts as a single bar and spreads toward the theoretical curve through collisions (Boltzmann’s H-theorem).

What is measured. The box is divided into n×n cells, and the Shannon entropy H = −Σ pi ln pi is computed from the fraction pi of particles in each cell. For large N, ln W ≈ N·H, so the two graphs above show the same information, normalized (H/Hmax) and unnormalized (ln W). The cell size (coarse-graining) defines “which macrostates are distinguished”, so it is normal for the curves to differ between grids.
Why isn’t the dashed line at 1? With a finite sample, the plug-in entropy comes out lower by about (M−1)/(2N) on average (M is the number of cells; the Miller–Madow correction term). For example, with N=180 and an 8×8 grid, H/Hmax≈0.95 even at equilibrium. The dashed line on this page is computed from an exact binomial sum, not an approximation. At high density the hard core suppresses density fluctuations, so the true equilibrium line may sit slightly above the dashed line. Check this with the “Restart randomly” control.
Physics model. Newtonian mode uses elastic hard disks of equal mass. The collision instant is computed exactly within a step (the contact time τ is found by solving a quadratic, the particles are rewound, and the normal velocity components are exchanged), so momentum and energy are conserved, and wall reflection is a reversible mirror reflection. It is not a fully event-driven scheme, so multiple collisions overlapping within one step are approximated; increasing the collision passes removes almost all residual overlap (2 passes: 0.02% overlapping pairs even at 100,000 particles and high density, maximum penetration about 7%). Reducing dt lowers the error further.
Velocity reversal (Loschmidt). After reversal, the entropy retraces its path back down. However, because of chaos, errors grow exponentially, so the longer you wait before reversing, the less well the initial state is recovered, and the entropy soon rises again. Increasing the perturbation ε makes this breakdown come much sooner. It is an experiment that lets you see Boltzmann’s interpretation that “entropy increase is not a law but overwhelming probability”.
Try turning collisions off. The gas becomes ideal and the speed distribution stays at its initial value (it never reaches thermal equilibrium). Positional entropy, however, still rises through wall reflections alone. In other words, the increase in positional entropy and the thermalization of the speed distribution are different processes.
Limitations. As the packing fraction approaches φ≈0.70 (the hard-disk crystallization region), the system is no longer a simple fluid. For this reason the upper limit of N is capped at φ≤0.55. The stochastic random-walk mode is for comparison, and relaxation becomes very slow as the radius gets smaller. The irreversibility in this mode is produced by injecting random numbers, not by the dynamics.
