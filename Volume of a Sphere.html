5. Cylindrical Shell Method

Spin a half-circle around the y-axis, stacking thin cylindrical shells

The question this method answersHow do you account for shells farther from the axis having a longer circumference?

A thin cylindrical shell of radius t has volume 2πt·(height)·dt. Push t from 0 to r, and the shells stack up to fill the sphere.

6. Kepler's Infinitesimal Pyramids

Slice the sphere's surface finely and fill it with pyramids to the center

The question this method answersIf you know only the surface area, can you get the volume?

A pyramid connecting a small surface patch dA to the center has volume ⅓·dA·r. Fill the whole surface with such pyramids and V = ⅓·(4πr²)·r = ⁴⁄₃πr³.
