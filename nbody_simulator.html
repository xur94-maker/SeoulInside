Reflecting current research: Butterfly, Moth and Bumblebee are among the 13 new periodic solutions discovered by Šuvakov & Dmitrašinović (2013) using shape-sphere topological classification. Since then, Li & Liao's group has used supercomputers and artificial neural networks (2017–2025) to find ~600 new equal-mass orbits, ~130,000 unequal-mass orbits, ~300 free-fall orbits, and in 2025 alone 10,059 new three-dimensional periodic orbits (including 1,996 linearly stable ones and 273 "piano-trio" orbits) — showing that this remains a very active area of research.
