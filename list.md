[Seoul Inside] Rally Holds, Foreigners Flee: Inside Korea's Paradox Market
May 22, 2026 · KOSPI Close of Market
KOSPI closed Friday at 7,847.71 (+0.41%) — up 75% year-to-date, the best-performing major index on the planet. Yet foreign investors sold a net ₩1.93 trillion for the 12th straight session, with year-to-date outflows estimated at $58 billion, potentially the largest annual foreign exodus ever recorded.
The paradox resolves on closer inspection: foreigners are profit-taking on SK Hynix (which tripled on AI memory demand) while rotating into Samsung Electronics — not leaving Korea, but rebalancing. Meanwhile, domestic retail and institutions absorbed every won of selling, backed by ₩478 trillion in ETF assets and the newly launched government National Growth Fund.
KOSDAQ surged +4.72%, triggering a buy-side sidecar for the second consecutive day. Samsung briefly touched ₩303,500 — implying a $1 trillion market cap — before closing 2% lower.
The won weakened to 1,514 per dollar even as stocks rose: a structural disconnect explained by foreign repatriation flows that domestic buyers cannot offset in the FX market.
Nomura's target: 10,000–11,000.
