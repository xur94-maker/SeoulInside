# KOSPI Crosses 8,000 for the First Time — But the Real Story Is What Foreigners Did After 3 PM
B. Sun | Seoul Inside
May 26, 2026




Seoul Inside | Korea Markets | May 26, 2026

Key Takeaways
KOSPI closed at a historic 8,047.51 (+2.55%), briefly touching 8,094.90 intraday — the index’s first-ever close above 8,000

Institutions were the sole net buyers (₩910B–₩1.3T); foreigners reversed from a +₩776B intraday position to -₩184.1B at the close, extending their selling streak to 13 consecutive sessions

Foreign net selling over May 7–22 totaled ₩46.3T, concentrated almost entirely in Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix (82.9% combined) — analysts characterize this as sector rotation, not capital flight

Korea’s margin loan balance hit a record ₩36.4724T as of May 21; VKOSPI stood at 66.97 on May 22 — the highest volatility reading since October 2008

Wednesday brings the debut of 2x single-stock leveraged ETFs on Samsung and SK Hynix, with an estimated ₩4T in seed capital; the FSS has explicitly flagged these as a potential volatility accelerant

The headline writes itself: Korea’s benchmark KOSPI index closed above 8,000 for the first time in history on Tuesday, finishing at 8,047.51 (+199.80 pts, +2.55%). The index briefly touched an intraday high of 8,094.90 before pulling back into the close.

But the day’s most consequential story was not the number. It was the dramatic reversal that unfolded in the final 90 minutes of trading — and what it reveals about the structural fragility beneath this record-breaking rally.

The Backdrop: Three Catalysts Behind the Surge
1. U.S.-Iran ceasefire negotiations Hopes for a breakthrough in U.S.-Iran nuclear and ceasefire talks advanced meaningfully, compressing Middle East risk premiums. Oil prices stabilized, and capital that had been parked in safe-haven assets began rotating back into equities.

2. Symbolic milestones in the semiconductor pair Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix — which together accounted for 43% of KOSPI’s daily trading value — breached widely-watched psychological levels. Samsung crossed ₩300,000 (”300,000 Electronics,” or Samjeonnija in Korean market parlance), while SK Hynix crossed ₩2,000,000 (”2 Million Nix”) intraday. For context, these two names alone represent approximately 35–40% of KOSPI’s total market capitalization.

3. Leveraged ETF pre-positioning The market was already pricing in Wednesday’s (May 27) debut of single-stock 2x leveraged ETFs on Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix — products estimated to carry ~₩4T in combined seed capital at launch. Pre-positioning by ETF sponsors drove mechanical buying into both names ahead of the open.

Institutions: The Sole Bull
Institutions drove the rally. Their net buying ranged from ₩910B to ₩1.3T depending on the source and scope — the divergence reflects whether exchange-traded derivative positions were included in the tally. Within the institutional bloc, securities firms led with approximately ₩1.7T in net purchases, largely attributable to ETF-related inflows being executed ahead of Wednesday’s product launch. National pension funds (연기금), by contrast, were net sellers — a notable internal divergence that tempers the headline institutional figure.

By mid-afternoon, as foreign flows reversed, institutions were carrying the index alone.

Foreigners: A Morning Narrative That Did Not Hold
This is where Tuesday’s story becomes complicated — and where timing matters considerably.

At 11:32 AM, foreigners were net buyers of +₩776B. By 1:55 PM, they had expanded that position to +₩641B net. The mid-session narrative — foreigners returning after 12 days of consecutive selling — was compelling, and widely reported.

Then the afternoon happened.

By the 3:30 PM close, foreigners had reversed to net sellers of -₩184.1B, extending their selling streak to 13 consecutive trading sessions. The “reversal” narrative was premature.

Data note: All intraday figures cited above are time-stamped snapshots from real-time wire reports. The -₩184.1B figure represents the final KRX-confirmed closing tally (Newspim, published 16:15). Intraday flow data in Korean markets is notoriously subject to late-session revisions, a pattern Tuesday illustrated clearly.
